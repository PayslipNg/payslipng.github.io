How it's calculated

  1. Gross pay = basic + housing + transport + other allowances. Annual gross = monthly gross × 12.
  2. Eligible deductions (Nigeria Tax Act 2025, section 30(2)): employee pension under the Pension Reform Act (default 8% of basic + housing + transport), NHF (default 2.5% of basic), NHIS contributions, owner-occupied mortgage interest, life insurance premiums, and rent relief of 20% of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000. The old Consolidated Relief Allowance no longer applies.
  3. Chargeable income = annual gross − eligible deductions.
  4. Annual tax (Fourth Schedule): first ₦800,000 at 0%; next ₦2,200,000 at 15%; next ₦9,000,000 at 18%; next ₦13,000,000 at 21%; next ₦25,000,000 at 23%; above ₦50,000,000 at 25%.
  5. Monthly PAYE = annual tax ÷ 12. Remit by the 10th of the following month to the tax authority of the employee's state of residence.
  6. Employer pension (default 10%) is a cost to the employer and is not deducted from the employee.

Sources: Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (Act No. 7, Official Gazette No. 117, 26 June 2025), section 30 and Fourth Schedule; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, "Nigeria – Individual – Sample personal income tax calculation" (reviewed 29 May 2026), which our test suite reproduces exactly (₦25m gross, ₦2m rent → PAYE ₦4,176,000); Joint Tax Board 2026 Personal Income Tax Guidelines for remittance and filing dates.
